## Conclusion 

  The GDG on Campus AITR website is well-built, with no broken links and good overall functionality on 
  laptop and mobile. The issues found are minor: uneven dot sizes in the heading, a misaligned dot on 
  mobile, the “View Details” and “View Full Team & Faculty” buttons landing at the bottom of the page 
  on mobile, and an already-conducted workshop still showing “6 seats left”. Fixing these and adding 
  more chapter lead details and advisor contact information would make the site more polished and 
  informative.
